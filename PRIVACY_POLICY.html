Last Updated: June 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Daily Wisdom Calm ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application ("App").

The App is designed for personal mindfulness, reflection, and well-being. It provides general inspirational content and is not intended as medical, psychological, or religious advice.

2. Information We Collect

```

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Mood Responses: Your answers to in-app questions used to personalize displayed content.
  • User Preferences: App settings such as notifications, themes, and sound preferences.
  • Usage Streaks: Information about daily engagement within the app.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Basic technical data required for app functionality (e.g., device type, OS version).

  • To improve overall app functionality and user experience.

4. Data Storage and Processing

```

4.1 Local Storage

4.2 Cloud Media Delivery

4.3 Security
